![title]
Energy Bridge
 
![brief]
Energy Bridges convert Imag Energy (IF) to FE and back.
 
![content]
The FE Input Bridge receives FE and converts it to IF. The FE Output Bridge receives IF and converts it to FE for adjacent devices.

Connect the bridge to an Imag Energy node using its wireless configuration page, and place an FE device or cable beside it.

Conversion rate: 1 IF = 4 FE.
