AcademyCraft TMRespawn

TMRespawn 1.0.5 release modifications, 2026-11-11, by lyanxk and TMRespawn contributors:
Adjusted default level-five CP and trainable CP/overload capacity limits, and
the CP recovery rate in the bundled gameplay datapack.

TMRespawn 1.0.4 release modifications, 2026-11-11, by lyanxk and TMRespawn contributors:
Adjusted skill damage balance and maximum-health scaling, with minor Teleporter
skill changes to range, damage, cooldowns and multi-item attacks. Updated related
effects, payloads and datapack parameters.

TMRespawn 1.0.3 release modifications, 2026-11-10, by lyanxk and TMRespawn contributors:
Extensively revised Meltdowner skills and migrated legacy 1.12.2 gameplay
configuration, skill balance, machine recipes, world generation and related
gameplay definitions to reloadable datapack resources with client synchronization.

TMRespawn 1.0.2 release modifications, 2026-11-10, by lyanxk and TMRespawn contributors:
Extensively revised Electromaster skills, including level enhancements, charging,
magnetic movement and manipulation, ore detection, body intensification, electric
attacks, Railgun and Thunder Clap. Enhanced skills retain prerequisite mastery
while their enhanced effects are retrained. Updated related visual effects.

TMRespawn 1.0.1 release modifications, 2026-11-10, by lyanxk and TMRespawn contributors:
Reworked Vector Manipulation skills for the TMC server, merging the former
Directed Blast entity attack into Directed Shock, separating large-area block
destruction into Directed Blast, and strengthening high-level abilities.
The full GNU GPL version 3 text is provided in COPYING.
